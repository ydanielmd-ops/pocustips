Yonathan Daniel, MD

POCUS Tips is a bedside point-of-care ultrasound reference covering the core cardiac, lung and IVC views used in acute care: how to get each view, what normal and abnormal look like, common pitfalls, and what a finding changes about management.

It's maintained as a teaching aid and quick-reference guide, built from standard bedside ultrasound teaching. More about the author and other work is at yonathan.online.

Educational use only. Nothing on this site is intended for diagnosis or treatment decisions — confirm findings with formal imaging and clinical judgment.
